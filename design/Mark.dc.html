What changed
01
Baselines aligned
The V sat lower than the wordmark, so the lockup read as two pieces. Both now sit on one baseline and the rule spans the full cap height.
02
Tracking put in order
Wordmark and tagline were tracked almost identically, so they competed. The wordmark keeps its wide setting; the tagline steps back to 0.30em and drops in weight.
03
A cut that survives small sizes
Didone hairlines disappear below roughly 24px — in a browser tab the V would have broken up. A second, heavier optical cut takes over at small sizes.
04
Stated as a system
Lockups, reversal, palette and type scale, so the mark behaves the same everywhere instead of being redrawn per screen.
